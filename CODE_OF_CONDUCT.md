
# Code of Conduct

## GraySentinel Cyber Defence Lab

**Learn Together. Build Together. Prove Together.**

The GraySentinel SOC Analyst Roadmap is an educational and collaborative cybersecurity project.

We welcome people with different backgrounds, experience levels and perspectives who want to learn, contribute and improve practical cybersecurity knowledge.

Our community is built on respect, responsible conduct, technical integrity and evidence-based learning.

This Code of Conduct applies to everyone participating in this repository and its associated project spaces.

---

# 1. Our Standards

We expect all community members to:

- Treat others with respect and professionalism.
- Communicate constructively.
- Welcome beginners and experienced professionals.
- Give feedback that improves the work rather than attacks the contributor.
- Respect different opinions and technical approaches.
- Credit original authors and sources.
- Share accurate information to the best of their knowledge.
- Be transparent about mistakes and limitations.
- Respect privacy and confidentiality.
- Follow applicable laws and cybersecurity safety requirements.

A person's experience level, educational background, employment status or contribution history must never be used to justify disrespectful treatment.

---

# 2. Expected Behaviour

Examples of positive community behaviour include:

### Respectful Communication

- Use professional and inclusive language.
- Ask questions without humiliating others.
- Disagree with technical ideas respectfully.
- Explain criticism with supporting reasoning.
- Accept that people may have different levels of knowledge.

### Constructive Collaboration

- Help others understand difficult concepts.
- Share useful learning resources.
- Review contributions objectively.
- Provide specific and actionable feedback.
- Acknowledge the work of others.

### Technical Integrity

- Do not fabricate test results.
- Do not claim someone else's work as your own.
- Disclose relevant limitations.
- Correct inaccurate information when identified.
- Respect software licences and intellectual property rights.

### Responsible Security Practice

- Work only within authorised environments.
- Do not share stolen credentials or confidential information.
- Report security concerns responsibly.
- Avoid exposing others to unnecessary security risks.

---

# 3. Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviour is not acceptable:

- Harassment, bullying or intimidation.
- Discrimination or hateful conduct.
- Personal attacks and targeted humiliation.
- Threats or encouragement of violence.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Deliberate disruption of community discussions.
- Repeated abusive or hostile communication.
- Impersonation or deliberate misrepresentation.
- Plagiarism and intentional misattribution.
- Fabrication of technical evidence or project achievements.
- Sharing confidential data without authorisation.
- Promoting or facilitating unauthorised cyber activity.

Technical disagreement is welcome. Personal abuse is not.

---

# 4. Cybersecurity Community Responsibilities

Because this is a cybersecurity-focused project, community members have additional responsibilities.

Do not use the community or repository to:

- Coordinate attacks against third-party systems.
- Share unauthorised access credentials.
- Publish private datasets or confidential logs.
- Distribute malicious payloads for real-world abuse.
- Encourage unlawful exploitation.
- Expose personal or organisational information.
- Present unauthorised security testing as legitimate research.

Educational demonstrations must use authorised, isolated or otherwise safe environments.

When in doubt, stop the activity and seek clarification before proceeding.

---

# 5. Reporting Unacceptable Behaviour

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, report it to the GraySentinel repository maintainers.

Use a private communication channel where possible rather than escalating the situation through a public argument.

When reporting an incident, include:

- A description of what happened.
- Relevant dates and approximate times.
- Links or screenshots where appropriate.
- The repository issue, pull request or discussion involved.
- Any immediate safety or privacy concerns.

Please avoid sharing unrelated personal information.

For security vulnerabilities or sensitive technical disclosures, follow the separate reporting instructions in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

---

# 6. Review and Enforcement

GraySentinel maintainers are responsible for reviewing reports and determining appropriate action.

Depending on the circumstances, actions may include:

1. A private reminder of community expectations.
2. A formal warning.
3. Removal of inappropriate content.
4. Temporary restrictions on participation.
5. Closure of discussions or contributions.
6. Removal from project spaces.
7. Permanent exclusion from the community.

Serious incidents may require immediate action.

Maintainers will consider the available evidence, severity, context, repeated behaviour and potential impact when deciding how to respond.

Retaliation against someone who reports a concern in good faith is not acceptable.

---

# 7. Fairness and Accountability

This Code of Conduct applies equally to:

- Repository owners.
- Maintainers.
- Contributors.
- Students.
- Working professionals.
- Volunteers.
- Community participants.

No individual is exempt from these standards because of their role, seniority, technical ability or relationship with GraySentinel.

Maintainers are also expected to act responsibly, communicate decisions professionally and avoid using their authority to intimidate or unfairly target contributors.

---

# 8. Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within:

- This GitHub repository.
- Repository issues and pull requests.
- Project discussions.
- Official project communication spaces.
- Other spaces where an individual is officially representing this project.

It applies whether participation is voluntary, educational or part of an independently agreed engagement.

---

# 9. Continuous Improvement

Community standards may evolve as the project grows.

GraySentinel may revise this document to address new risks, improve reporting procedures or clarify expectations.

The latest version in this repository will be the reference for participation in this project.

---

# 10. Our Commitment

We want this repository to be a place where people can:

- Ask questions without fear of humiliation.
- Learn through practical work.
- Challenge technical ideas respectfully.
- Share evidence honestly.
- Improve through constructive feedback.
- Build cybersecurity knowledge responsibly.

A strong technical community is built not only through what its members know, but also through how they work with one another.

**Respect the people. Verify the work. Protect the trust.**
