# Code of Conduct

We want this project to be a respectful, inclusive and constructive learning environment.

- Be respectful and professional.
- Critique ideas and work, not people.
- Give useful, evidence-based feedback.
- Respect different experience levels.
- Do not harass, discriminate, threaten or humiliate others.
- Do not publish private information or confidential material.
- Do not misuse project access or perform unauthorised security testing.

Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation after a fair review of serious or repeated violations. Contributors may raise concerns through the repository's GitHub contact or issue mechanisms.
