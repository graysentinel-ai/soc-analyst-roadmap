## 9. Responsible Use of Resources

Before using external tools, datasets, documentation or software:

- Verify that the source is legitimate.
- Check the latest available version.
- Review licensing and usage conditions.
- Follow applicable terms of service.
- Use tools only in authorized environments.
- Avoid downloading untrusted files or scripts.
- Never expose credentials or sensitive information.

Third-party resources are provided for educational reference. Their inclusion does not imply endorsement by GraySentinel.
